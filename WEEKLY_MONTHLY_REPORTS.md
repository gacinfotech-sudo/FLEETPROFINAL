```

### Monthly Reports
```
1st Monday 09:00 UTC - Monthly review meeting
1st Monday 10:00 UTC - Capacity planning session
1st Monday 11:00 UTC - Security review
1st Monday 14:00 UTC - Stakeholder presentation
```

### Quarterly Reviews
```

Attendees: All stakeholders
```

### Annual Review
```
